Having started learning Spanish earlier last year, it only made sense to add Catalan to the mix 🇦🇩

I live in a place where both languages coexist daily, so picking it up feels less like a study project and more like... an inevitability. And as it turns out, Catalan sits in a sweet spot for me: it shares a lot with Spanish, but also – perhaps surprisingly – with Italian, which makes the transfer feel almost intuitive at times.

I've also been reading a brilliant book, *The Seven Sieves: How To Read All The Romance Languages Right Away*, which breaks down the systematic sound shifts and structural patterns across the Romance family and gives you concrete tools to leverage what you already know. Highly recommended if you're into this kind of thing.

So: tres llengües, un cervell, and hopefully enough coffee to keep it all straight ☕
